## More Than Just a Cloud Resume

My journey isn't just about finishing a challenge; it's about mastering the stack. I’ve shifted my focus from just "making it work" to "making it secure and scalable."

### Current Milestones:
* **Infrastructure as Code:** Moving away from the Azure Portal and strictly using **Terraform**.
* **Identity Management:** Hardening **Entra ID** (Azure AD) and enforcing MFA.
* **Observability:** Visualizing threat telemetry with **Splunk**.

The "Cloud Resume Challenge" was the catalyst, but the journey to becoming a Senior Architect starts with these small, automated steps.
